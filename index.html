Products

Every product and every variety, in one place.

One tomato entry holds all its selling units. Change a price once and the storefront updates everywhere it appears.

  • Sell the same item in 1 kg, 500 g and 250 g — each with its own price.
  • Cost, MRP and your margin sit on every unit, so you always see what you earn.
  • Out of stock this morning? Switch the item off with one tap, switch it back tomorrow.
  • Combo kits and bundles live beside loose produce, not in a separate list.
  • Today's mandi rate shows up on the screen while you edit prices.

Orders

Sort the day's orders by area before you load the vehicle.

Pick an area and you see only those orders — so one round covers one locality instead of crossing the city twice.

  • Area filter by locality — Budhwar Peth, Shaniwar Peth and the rest — with distance shown on each order.
  • Household orders and Restaurant & Hotel orders stay in separate tabs — bulk buyers never get mixed with home deliveries.
  • Follow each order through Placed, Accepted and Out for Delivery without a diary.
  • Payment mode on every order, so you know what is COD to collect and what is already paid by UPI.
  • Call the customer straight from the order card.

Stock

The app writes your mandi list for you.

It reads the orders that came in, adds them up item by item, adds a small buffer, and hands you a list you can shop from. Move the slider to see it work.

Same calculation runs for every item you sell — onion, palak, mango, combo kits. You reach the mandi already knowing the quantities.

  • Mark items To Buy or Purchased as you move through the yard.
  • Urgent items and pre-orders are flagged so nothing gets missed.
  • Live inventory count with a warning on whatever is running out.

Offers

Run your own offers and banners, without calling us.

Set a discount in the morning, take it off in the evening. The homepage banner is yours to change whenever you like.

  • Spot discounts, festive deals and combo offers, each with a switch to turn it on or off.
  • Your margin after discount is shown while you set the price — no surprises at month end.
  • Homepage banners you upload and reorder yourself.
  • Set an expiry on festive offers and they end on their own, even if you forget.

Analytics

Know how the business did, without opening a register.

One screen for today, the last seven days or the full month — and a report you can export whenever you need it.

  • Orders completed, with your daily average.
  • Revenue, net profit and the margin you are actually working on.
  • Customer behaviour — how many buyers come back, and how many are loyal regulars.
  • Weekly sales trend, so you can see growth week over week and spot your peak day.
  • Household versus restaurant split, to see which side of the business is growing.

Customer-facing app

Now, what your customers see when they open Kisan Pharma.

Same shop, same stock, a different screen. This is the app your customers use to browse, order and pay — five screens, shaped to turn a visit into a sale.

  • Marathi from the first screen
  • Built to convert cart to order
  • UPI & Cash on Delivery
Language Home Offers Cart Profile

First launch

Customers pick their language before anything else.

The very first screen a new visitor sees. One choice, made once — everything after it follows in the language they're comfortable reading.

  • Two options only — English or मराठी — nothing to configure or search for.
  • Menus, product names, offers and checkout all follow whichever one they pick.
  • Returning customers skip this screen entirely; their choice is remembered.
  • Removes the biggest barrier for customers who read Marathi more comfortably than English.

Home

They see what's fresh, and exactly how long they have to order it.

A live cutoff time for tomorrow's delivery sits right under the search bar, so nobody has to wonder if they've missed the window.

  • "Order before 11 PM for tomorrow's delivery" — the next slot, stated plainly, before they even scroll.
  • A seasonal banner promotes whatever you're pushing — mango season, a festival combo, a weekend special.
  • Two clear categories, Fresh Vegetables and Pre-Cut & Ready, so ready-to-cook shoppers aren't digging through raw produce.
  • Search by typing, voice, or barcode — fast for customers reordering the same staples.
  • Original price shown struck through next to the discounted one, on every product card.

Offers

The deals you set on your side are what sell your stock.

Whatever you switch on in your Offers screen appears here, live, styled to actually get tapped — a countdown, a coupon code, a deal of the day.

  • A running countdown on time-bound sales creates a reason to buy today, not "later."
  • Coupon codes customers copy in one tap and apply at checkout.
  • Deal of the Day spotlights whatever you're trying to move fastest — with the exact rupee saving shown.
  • One place for you to manage offers; instantly live for every customer browsing.

Cart & checkout

Built to turn a full cart into a placed order.

Every step between "add to cart" and "pay" is designed to remove hesitation — the delivery slot is confirmed up front, savings stay visible, and paying takes one tap.

  • Delivery address, day and time slot confirmed before payment — no surprise later.
  • A progress bar nudges customers toward free delivery: "add ₹160 more for FREE delivery."
  • Total savings shown in green, right beside the amount they're actually paying.
  • Pay online by UPI, card or wallet, or choose Cash on Delivery — their choice, not forced.

Profile

Reordering takes one tap, not a new search.

Most weeks look like the last one. Reorder puts last week's basket straight back in the cart, so repeat customers barely open the product list.

  • One-tap Reorder from the most recent order.
  • Order history, saved addresses and payment methods, all in one place.
  • Wallet balance and total saved, so loyal customers can see the value they're getting.
  • Direct WhatsApp chat to your store for anything the app can't answer.
